Bible Origins: Support

Bible Origins is a free Bible reader that shows how different Bible texts word the same verse.

What the colours mean

Gold: the translations word it differently. Blue: the ancient Hebrew or Greek texts themselves differ; each blue item is written up with references. Purple: words only the Aramaic Targum adds (switch it on in Reading style → Compare with). Different wording is not a contradiction, and no Bible in the app is marked as the correct one.

Where the texts come from

Every text, with its edition, licence and source link, is listed in the app: Home → Credits & Sources.
