Bible Origins: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

Bible Origins collects very little, and nothing about what you read.

There is no account and no sign-in, and there are no ads in the app.

Ad measurement (Meta). To see whether our ads on Facebook and Instagram work, the app (from version 1.4) tells Meta Platforms when it is installed or opened, and when a free trial or subscription starts, with its price and currency. It is sent with basic device and app details (such as the phone model, iOS version and app version) that Meta needs to count it. We do not ask to track you, we do not use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and nothing about your reading, notes or searches is ever sent. Meta handles this information under its own privacy policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Every Bible text is stored inside the app, so reading and comparing work with no internet connection.

Your notes, your place in each book and your reading settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

The source links on the app's Credits & Sources screen open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Questions: see the support page.
