Bible Origins: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Bible Origins does not collect any data.

There is no account and no sign-in. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

Every Bible text is stored inside the app, so reading and comparing work with no internet connection. The app makes no network requests.

Your notes, your place in each book and your reading settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

The source links on the app's Credits & Sources screen open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Questions: see the support page.
