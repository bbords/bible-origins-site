Bible Origins: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Bible Origins collects very little, and nothing about what you read.

There is no account and no sign-in, and there are no ads in the app.

Ad measurement (Meta and TikTok). To see whether our ads on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok work, the app tells Meta Platforms (from version 1.4) and TikTok (from version 1.6) when it is installed or opened, and when a free trial, subscription or gift purchase starts, with its price and currency. It is sent with basic device and app details (such as the phone model, iOS version and app version) that they need to count it. From version 1.6 Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether you allow tracking: only if you allow it is your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) sent with these events, so the ad that brought you to the app can be credited. If you don't allow it, nothing in the app changes. Nothing about your reading, notes or searches is ever sent, and no names or email addresses. Meta and TikTok handle this information under their own privacy policies: facebook.com/privacy/policy and tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Every Bible text is stored inside the app, so reading and comparing work with no internet connection.

Your notes, your place in each book and your reading settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

The source links on the app's Credits & Sources screen open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Questions: see the support page.
